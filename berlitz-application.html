How I'd work

The approach I'd bring to Enterprise Products

01

Map the real stakeholders

Identify who buys, who administers and who uses the tool daily — and where their needs actually conflict.

02

Design the system before the screens

Component-driven, token-based systems that hold up as the product grows past the first release.

03

Work the data model with engineering

Sit inside how the underlying data is structured, not just how it's displayed on screen.

04

Ship it, then maintain it as infrastructure

Treat the design system as living infrastructure to steward, not a one-off deliverable to hand over.

I'd bring the same instinct to Berlitz: comfortable owning ambiguity, biased toward systems that scale rather than one-off screens, and an AI-native workflow that fits how modern product teams already work.

What I'd bring on day one:

  • End-to-end ownership across every surface
  • Systems maintained as infrastructure
  • Direct partnership with engineering on data models
  • AI-native execution, daily
