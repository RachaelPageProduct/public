TickLists — Internal brand reference
Last updated September 2026
01

Logo

TickLists has one logo — the organic blob mark with "TickLists.app" lettering — output in two colour renditions for contrast: pale-yellow-on-teal for in-app use, teal-deep-on-pale-yellow for the favicon and app icon. It is not used inline in page headers — List View, Bulk Upload, and Account headers show only a back button and the screen/list title text, no mark.

Redesigned Aug 2026: The old circle-track-and-checkmark mark has been fully retired in favour of the organic blob mark below. Assets referenced here are pulled live from ticklists.app — if either file moves, update the src paths.
Primary logo — in-app rendition
Pale-yellow mark, teal wordmark. Source: ticklists.app/img/ticklists logo lime round.svg
Favicon / app icon rendition
Teal-deep + pale-yellow — inverted for contrast at small sizes (browser tab, home-screen icon).
Same favicon asset on light — its own teal-deep background carries the contrast either way.
Where the logo actually appears in-app
My Lists
Standalone, centred above the heading — only on the loading screen and the top of the My Lists page. Also appears small (26px) inside the "Add to Home Screen" prompt badge. Not used on List View, Bulk Upload, or Account — those headers are title-text only.
No inline lockup: unlike the old design, the mark is never placed inline beside a list title inside a header. Where it appears, it's the standalone full logo, centred, above the text.

Don'ts
TickLists
Don't recolour the logo. Only approved colour combinations.
Don't stretch or distort the mark. Scale proportionally only.
TickLists
Don't place the logo on unauthorised background colours. Use only teal, teal deep, white, or light grey.
Don't add drop shadows or effects to the mark.
TickLists
Don't use text spelling "TickLists" next to the mark — the mark is standalone or paired with a list title only.
Don't use the logo at low opacity.
